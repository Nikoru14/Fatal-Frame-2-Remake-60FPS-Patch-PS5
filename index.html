How to use

  1. Make sure your game is on version 01.003.004.
  2. Put the file in /data/OnionHEN/cheats/ on your jailbroken PS5.
  3. Launch the game and enable 60fps (experimental) from your cheat menu.
  4. To go back to 30fps, untick it.

Heads up

  • Version-locked. A game update moves the offsets, and the wrong version can crash the game.
  • Tested in gameplay only. Cutscenes were made for 30fps, so check them and the Camera Obscura for odd timing.
  • For jailbroken consoles and your own copy of the game.
